WHITE NOISE

A frictionless interface for a signal-only AI workspace.

We removed traditional frames and chrome, designing a typography-first surface where hierarchy comes from motion, spacing, and light shifts rather than color.

  • Non-intrusive system status language
  • Ambient motion for focus & flow
  • Responsive grid tuned for dense inputs
Prototype ML tooling

SIGNAL ATLAS

Research workflows that keep humans in the loop without friction.

Atlas organizes long-running investigations into manageable rituals: snapshotting, sense-making, and quick triage of outliers.

  • Timeline view for narrative debugging
  • Rapid bookmarking from keyboard
  • Conversation-aware comments

NEBULA FRAME

A modular dashboard language for volatile, high-frequency data.

Nebula Frame treats each metric as a moving actor in a shared field, rather than a chart on a wall. Components reflow as conditions change, preserving orientation and context.

  • Component library for live data tiles
  • Motion rules for entering / exiting metrics
  • Dark and light modes sharing one structural logic
